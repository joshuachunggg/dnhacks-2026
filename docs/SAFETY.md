# Safety

## Claim boundary
- The app may explain, compare, and estimate.
- The app may not claim professional electrical sign-off.
- The app may not imply hidden wiring is known from an image alone.
- The app may not present uncertain observations as confirmed facts.

## Prohibited consumer actions
- Do not coach a homeowner to remove an electrical panel dead front.
- Do not coach exposure to energized conductors.
- Do not instruct unsafe electrical work.
- Do not recommend that a consumer bypass code, permitting, or inspection requirements.

## Professional verification required
Route to electrician or AHJ verification when:
- hidden conductor condition is unknown,
- service size is uncertain,
- panel compatibility is unclear,
- grounding or bonding is not safely visible,
- local permit or inspection requirements are unresolved,
- the result depends on professional judgment.

## Language rules
- Use `proposed`, `observed`, `inferred`, `calculated`, and `professionally_verified` explicitly.
- Separate assumptions from evidence.
- Separate source-backed regulatory facts from sample demo data.
- Say `insufficient data` rather than pretending certainty.

## Demo disclaimer template
"This assessment is preliminary and based on visible evidence, user input, and deterministic checks. Final electrical decisions require a licensed electrician or the authority having jurisdiction."
