# Austin, TX EVSE demo card

Retrieval date: 2026-09-05

## Jurisdiction card
- Austin Energy says hard-wired home charging stations and receptacles for plug-in stations require a City of Austin electrical permit and inspection.[1]
- Austin Development Services says the FY 2025-26 fee schedule went into effect on Oct. 1, 2025.[2]

## Cost assumptions for the demo
- Baseline assumption: simple residential EVSE work stays within the standard City of Austin electrical permit / inspection path; do not assume Austin Energy service work unless the project scope explicitly requires it.[1][3]
- Cost anchor: use the FY 2026 homeowner permitting fee table’s $166.99 residential electric fee as a placeholder only when the permit scope matches that residential fee table.[3]
- Escalation assumption: if the install needs a panel upgrade or larger meter, Austin Energy notes a $200 Electric Service Planning Application Fee; if the work requires distribution-system upgrades, line-extension work is at estimated cost.[3]

## Limitations
- This is a narrow demo fixture, not legal advice or a permit determination.
- Exact fees depend on scope and whether the charger install triggers service or line-extension work.
- The $166.99 figure is a general residential electric-fee anchor, not a universal EVSE total.[3]
